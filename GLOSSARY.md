# Batman

Batman is a personal collection of skills and notes for working with AI. These terms describe the UI exploration workflow currently under discussion.

## Language

**UI prototype**:
A disposable, interactive alternative for exploring a proposed feature with mock data. Its immediate purpose is to help a team discuss and agree on the experience and scope to build.
_Avoid_: Production feature, MVP

**Team alignment**:
Agreement within the team about the intended feature experience and MVP scope. This agreement does not establish how users will respond to the released feature.
_Avoid_: User validation

**User evidence**:
Observations of user behavior or feedback that inform whether a feature meets user needs. In the proposed workflow, this usually comes from analytics and feedback after release.
_Avoid_: Team preference
